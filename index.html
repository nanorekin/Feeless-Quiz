How to play

Goal

Answer crypto questions before the 15-second timer runs out. Faster correct answers score more, and a streak of correct answers adds a bonus. Press 1 to 4 on a keyboard to answer, then Enter for the next question.

Scoring

A correct answer is worth 100 points, plus up to 50 for speed and up to 50 for your streak. Wrong answers and timeouts score nothing and reset your streak.

Modes

Daily: 10 questions, the same for everyone, new at 00:00 UTC. Finish it to keep your daily streak. Quick 10: a fresh random round. Survival: keep going until you have made 3 mistakes.

50/50

Remove two wrong answers once per round. Supporters get two per round.

Ranks

Your total correct answers earn a rank: Newbie, Hodler (25), Diamond Hands (100), Whale (250) and Satoshi (500).

About the questions

Questions cover Nano, Bitcoin, Ethereum, other coins, key ideas, security and crypto history. Each answer comes with a short explanation. This is for fun and learning, not financial advice.

Supporters

A tip of 0.01 XNO or more unlocks the extra accent colours and a second 50/50. Tips are optional and the whole game is free.
